Why ple?

The industry is using AI to generate code. We think that's a trap. Generating code just generates technical debt faster. ple is built on three unyielding pillars:

1. Pure Intent

Speak the domain, not the database. You shouldn't have to translate your business rules into relational tables or HTTP controllers. With ple, your plain-text Behavior-Driven Design (BDD) specifications are the only source of truth.

2. Deterministic Compilation

Math, not probability. We use Large Language Models strictly to understand your fuzzy human intent. Once we map that to our rigid Abstract Domain Model, the AI is removed. Our compiler generates your backend using 100% deterministic math. Zero hallucinations in your execution layer.

3. Event Sourced by Default

Zero migration debt. We explicitly reject CRUD. If your business rules change, you don't write complex SQL migrations. You update your intent, throw the old codebase away, compile a new one, and replay your immutable event log. Code becomes truly disposable.
